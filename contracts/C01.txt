SAAS SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This SaaS Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date 15 February 2026 between Pinnacle Infotech Pvt Ltd ("Customer") and CloudNine Solutions Inc ("Supplier").

1. TERM. This Agreement shall be effective for a period of (24) months from the Effective Date.

2. RENEWAL. This Agreement shall not automatically renew. Either party may propose renewal by written notice at least thirty (30) days before the end of the term.

3. PAYMENT. Customer shall pay all undisputed invoices within (30) days of receipt of the invoice.

4. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY. Supplier's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed INR 50,00,000 (Fifty Lakh) for all claims arising under or in connection with this Agreement.

5. INDEMNITY. Each party shall indemnify the other against all losses, damages and costs arising from a breach of this Agreement or negligence of the indemnifying party.

6. TERMINATION. Either party may terminate this Agreement only for material breach that remains uncured for thirty (30) days after written notice.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY. Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party that is marked as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.

8. DATA PROTECTION. Each party shall comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and all applicable data protection laws. Supplier, as data processor, shall process personal data only as instructed by Customer and shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures.

9. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Singapore.

10. DELIVERABLES. Supplier shall deliver initial platform configuration on or before 15 April 2026.

11. DELIVERABLES. Customer shall provide access credentials and data migration files on or before March 15, 2026.

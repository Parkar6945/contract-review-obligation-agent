SAAS SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This SaaS Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date 05 January 2026 between Meridian Pharma Ltd ("Customer") and NovaTech Systems ("Supplier").

1. TERM. This Agreement shall be effective for a period of (36) months from the Effective Date.

2. RENEWAL. This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods of the same length unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal not less than (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

3. PAYMENT. Customer shall pay all undisputed invoices within (30) days of receipt of the invoice.

4. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY. Supplier's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid by Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

5. INDEMNITY. Each party shall indemnify the other against all losses, damages and costs arising from a breach of this Agreement or negligence of the indemnifying party.

6. TERMINATION. Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other party.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY. Each party shall keep confidential all information received from the other party that is marked as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.

8. GENERAL PROVISIONS. Each party shall comply with all applicable laws in the performance of its obligations under this Agreement.

9. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India.

10. DELIVERABLES. Supplier shall deliver system integration documentation on or before 05 March 2026.

11. DELIVERABLES. Customer shall provide user acceptance test results on or before April 05, 2026.

12. DELIVERABLES. Supplier shall submit go-live readiness report on or before 05 June 2026.
